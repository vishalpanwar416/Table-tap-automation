Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 30, 2026

Welcome to AutoDM. Your privacy and trust are extremely important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, process, and protect your information when using our Instagram Comment-to-DM Automation service.

1. Information We Collect

We process minimal information required to execute requested Instagram automations:

  • Instagram Account Information: Account ID, handle, and public profile data authorized via Meta OAuth 2.0.
  • Comment Data: Public Instagram comment text posted on your content to identify trigger keywords.
  • Direct Messages: Automated response messages dispatched to user inboxes upon keyword triggers.

2. How We Use Information

All data is processed strictly to provide the core service functionality:

  • Detecting keyword triggers on your Instagram posts.
  • Sending automated direct messages requested by users.
  • Displaying live automation logs on your personal dashboard.

3. Data Protection & Sharing

We do not sell, lease, or share any personal user data or message history with third parties. All Meta Graph API access tokens are encrypted and stored securely.

4. Data Deletion & Revocation

You can revoke access to AutoDM at any time by removing the app connection in your Facebook Page Settings or Instagram App Permissions. To request complete data deletion, contact us below.
